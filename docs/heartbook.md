# Life OS — Heartbook

## Why we exist

Life is not lived in milestones. It is lived on ordinary Tuesdays.

Life OS exists to create a place where people can hear themselves again — not through productivity or optimization, but through attention, honest questions, remembering, and gentle observation.

The goal is not to manufacture a better version of a person. The goal is to help someone become more intimately acquainted with their own life.

## The central belief

> You are not a problem to solve. You are a human being to know.

Life OS should cultivate curiosity instead of criticism, awareness instead of judgment, choice instead of compulsion, and compassion instead of shame.

## The Hearth

Life OS should feel like home: a warm glow, a lit fireplace, chill in the air, leaves rustling in a light breeze.

The product should feel like somewhere a person can stop performing and tell the truth.

The app never shouts. It never grades humanity. It never punishes absence. It never pretends to know a person better than they know themselves.

Sometimes the kindest thing the app can do is tell the user to close it and go live.

## The Covenant

When you arrive here:

- You will not be graded.
- You will not be rushed.
- You will not be compared.
- You will not be optimized into somebody else's idea of a good life.
- You will be invited to notice, reflect, remember, and tell the truth.
- Some days you will have words. Some days you will not. Both are welcome.
- The system walks beside you, never in front of you.

## Human authority

Data may observe. It does not decide what a life means.

Life OS can say, “You often describe feeling alive after time outside.” It should then ask whether that observation feels true. The human gets the final vote.

## What can be measured

Numbers can reveal patterns that memory misses. Possible signals include energy, mood, aliveness, ease, stress, connection, sleep, movement, meaningful activity, creativity, nature, financial security, and career satisfaction.

But some things should remain words, memories, stories, and meaning rather than scores: love, grief, identity, awe, purpose, and beautiful moments.

## Remembrance

Grief is not an anomaly. It can be a continuing relationship.

Life OS should make room for people and animals who have died, birthdays and anniversaries, stories, photographs, voice notes, rituals, and simple remembrance without requiring someone to “move on.”

Sometimes no journaling is needed. Sometimes the correct interaction is simply: **light the lantern**.

## The Night Sky

Moments a person chooses to keep may become stars.

Brightness represents **meaning, not happiness**.

Over time, related moments can form constellations. Those constellations should be suggestions, never verdicts. The user may rename, redraw, reject, or reinterpret them.

The Night Sky should eventually allow a person to zoom from years of life, to constellations, to seasons, to individual moments.

## North Star

> Does this help someone build a wiser, kinder, more honest relationship with themselves?

If a feature is clever but not kind, it does not belong.

If it makes the Hearth bigger but not warmer, it does not belong.
